## Linking system

**Just link to the next thing.**

> telephone → sausage → monkey → button → book → cabbage → glass → mouse → stomach → cardboard →
> ferry → Christmas → athlete → key → wigwam → baby → kiwi → bed → paintbrush → walnut

Make a visual link from each word to the next one:
1. The picture should be **vivid** and **emotionally engaging**.
2. The **elements** of each picture should **interact**.
3. The picture should be **unusual**, not normal.

**Drawback:** if one connection is lost, the whole link is lost.

## The loci system

Loci mnemonics work by attaching images to places along a familiar real-life route you know well.

### Memory palaces

An example of applying the loci system.
- Go **clockwise**: start with something on your immediate left and work your way around.
- Add a **marker every five loci** on your system. E.g. to recall the twelfth, walk to the marker
  "ten" and then continue two more.

## The peg system

For remembering numbers.

| 1 bun | 2 shoe | 3 tree | 4 door | 5 hive | 6 sticks | 7 heaven | 8 gate | 9 line | 10 hen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Not always useful.* So turn 0–9 into common consonants instead:

| Digit | Letter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | z / s | z is in "zero", and s sounds similar to z |
| 1 | l | looks like 1 |
| 2 | n | two down strokes |
| 3 | m | three down strokes |
| 4 | r | fou**r** |
| 5 | f / v | **f**i**v**e |
| 6 | b / p | look similar to 6 |
| 7 | t | 7 and T look similar |
| 8 | ch / sh / j | the "gh" in eight, and j is near to these |
| 9 | g | looks alike |

**Be careful while adding filler words: they should not involve other digit letters.** Fillers
are vowels, h and y.

Examples:
- 1743 → LTRM → "Late Tram". ✎ "Late Tram" has two t's, so it strictly decodes to 17743. Brown's own
  words in the book are "Light Room" and "Le Tram", which decode to exactly 1743.
- 42293 → RNNGM → "Running Man". ✎ Read up to the M; the final n of "man" would add a 2 (422932).

## Memorising a deck of cards

- 9 of Hearts → 9H → H9 → H-g → **Hag**. Say it's at position 1 → a hag drinking ale.
- 6 of Spades → 6S → S6 → S-b → **Sob**. At position 2 (hen) → a sobbing hen.
- Issue with Jacks: J would be confused with j (8). So the Jack is a boy; the Jack of Hearts is Cupid.

## Remembering names

**Route 1**
1. Bring someone to mind who has the same name: a friend, relative or famous person.
2. Imagine that the person in front of you has been made up to look a bit like this other person,
   and is impersonating them.

**Route 2**
1. Connect the name with another image: "Mike" becomes a microphone, "Alice" becomes an Alice
   band, Bill → an invoice.
2. Find something memorable about the person's features, size or appearance. Something around the
   face is best and quickest. Use their clothing if something stands out. A beard, glasses, a large
   nose, a scar or mark, spiky hair. Maybe their face reminds you of someone, or of some particular
   animal.
3. Link the two together: Mike's large nose becomes a microphone stuck in the middle of his face.
   Imagine Bill putting on his small rimmed glasses to read the bill after dinner.
